Scope of Work (SOW)

CRM & Salon Management Web Application System

Comprehensive ERP & CRM

📌 Project Overview

Design and develop a centralized web-based platform integrating Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Salon Operations Management. The system streamlines customer handling, appointment scheduling, staff management, POS billing, inventory tracking, and marketing automation.

Core Modules Breakdown (10 Modules)

1
User Roles & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Admin: Full system control, branch management, salary settings
  • Manager: Daily operational control, inventory & reports access
  • Receptionist: Appointments, customer walk-in check-in, POS billing
  • Staff / Stylist: Limited access (assigned schedules & commission view)
  • Customer (Optional): Self-service online booking portal
  • Multi-Branch Support: Centralized multi-location salon management
2
Customer Relationship Management (CRM) & Lead System
  • Customer profiles (Name, Phone, Email, DOB, Anniversary)
  • Detailed visit history, preferred stylists & service notes
  • Lead capture from walk-ins, website, and social media
  • Lead status tracking (New, Contacted, Converted)
  • Automated follow-up reminders for receptionists
  • Customer segmentation based on visit frequency
3
Appointment & Calendar Booking System
  • Online self-booking & offline walk-in booking portal
  • Interactive calendar-based scheduling (Day/Week view)
  • Real-time staff availability & slot duration management
  • Auto appointment reminders (SMS/WhatsApp API integration)
  • One-click reschedule and cancellation functionality
  • Service buffer time and slot locking
4
Salon Service & Package Management
  • Comprehensive service catalog (Haircut, Spa, Facial, Color)
  • Dynamic pricing & service duration configuration
  • Service package creation (bundled combos & discounts)
  • Category-wise service classification
5
Billing & Point of Sale (POS) System
  • Fast invoice generation with print receipt support
  • Multiple payment modes (Cash, Card, UPI, Split Payment)
  • Discount coupon code integration & manual overrides
  • GST and tax rate configuration
  • Stylist tip and commission tag per bill
6
Inventory & Product Stock Management
  • Retail & internal usage product stock tracking
  • Supplier database & purchase order entry
  • Automated low stock alerts & minimum thresholds
  • Service-wise product consumption tracking
7
Loyalty & Membership Program
  • Points-based reward system (earn & redeem on billing)
  • Membership plans (Silver, Gold, Platinum tiers)
  • Referral program tracking & automated reward triggers
  • Member-only discount rules
8
Marketing Automation & Communication
  • Bulk SMS & WhatsApp promotional campaign manager
  • Automated birthday and anniversary offer triggers
  • Customer re-engagement campaigns for inactive users
  • Custom offer templates & delivery tracking
9
Reports & Executive Analytics
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly revenue performance reports
  • Customer retention & churn analytics
  • Staff performance & commission breakdown
  • Service popularity and peak-hours analytics
  • Inventory consumption & profit margin reports
10
Admin Panel & Security Settings
  • Full centralized system control & settings
  • User creation, role assignment, and access control
  • System backup, audit log, and security controls
  • Branch settings & multi-currency/tax settings
